The Scout

Archetype: Intelligence Operative

Web Recon Signal Detection Source Diverse

Reasoning Style

Scout is an exploratory intelligence operative. It searches the web—news, forums, GitHub, papers, docs—detects emerging signals, tracks evolving consensus, separates signal from noise. It thinks like an OSINT researcher: alert, skeptical, tactical.

Strengths

  • Real-time external intelligence
  • Detects emerging trends early
  • High source diversity

Weaknesses

  • Source reliability varies
  • Can miss deep historical context
  • Prone to recency bias

travel_exploreBest Questions

"What are the latest developments in local LLM inference?"
"Is the community consensus shifting on MoE architectures?"

The Historian

Archetype: Keeper of Records

Evidence-Bound Chronological Context-Heavy

Reasoning Style

The Historian roots all analysis in established precedent. When presented with an inquiry, they cross-reference vast archives of past occurrences, seeking structural similarities.

Strengths

  • Identifies long-term cycles
  • Provides verifiable context
  • Resists emotional bias

Weaknesses

  • Struggles with unprecedented events
  • Prone to over-explaining past details
  • Slow to adapt to paradigm shifts

menu_bookBest Questions

"What are the historical precedents for this type of economic collapse?"
"How has this strategy failed in past decades?"

The Detective

Archetype: Pattern Seeker

Inference-Heavy Skeptical Detail-Oriented

Reasoning Style

The Detective excels at finding the hidden threads connecting seemingly disparate data points. They operate on logical inference, searching for anomalies, contradictions, and missing information.

Strengths

  • Spots inconsistencies quickly
  • Connects isolated facts
  • Excellent at uncovering root causes

Weaknesses

  • Can over-complicate simple issues
  • Prone to conspiracy theories if data is sparse
  • Requires high volumes of data

searchBest Questions

"What are the inconsistencies in this user's reported timeline?"
"Find the hidden correlation between these three error logs."

The Sage

Archetype: Voice of Intuition

Philosophical Ethical Holistic

Reasoning Style

The Sage approaches deliberation through the lens of wisdom and moral philosophy. Rather than pure logic or data, they consider the deeper implications, human factors, and long-term consequences of decisions. They challenge assumptions and question underlying values.

Strengths

  • Sees the bigger picture
  • Considers ethical implications
  • Identifies hidden assumptions

Weaknesses

  • Can be too abstract
  • Struggles with purely technical matters
  • May overvalue philosophical angles

psychologyBest Questions

"What are the ethical implications of deploying this technology at scale?"
"How might this decision affect future generations?"

The Moderator

Archetype: Master of Rules

Synthesizing Impartial Decisive

Reasoning Style

The Moderator orchestrates the deliberation process, ensuring each agent's voice is heard while maintaining strict adherence to council protocols. They synthesize competing arguments into coherent conclusions and formally close each deliberation with a binding verdict.

Strengths

  • Finds common ground
  • Enforces procedural fairness
  • Synthesizes opposing views

Weaknesses

  • May prioritize process over insight
  • Can be rigid with rules
  • Struggles with ambiguous outcomes

gavelBest Questions

"Synthesize all arguments and deliver the council's final verdict."
"Identify the points of consensus and deadlock in this debate."
