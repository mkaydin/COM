Council of Minds

Where AI Minds Deliberate

Present a question and a council of specialized AI minds deliberates in parallel — each reasoning its own way, then the Moderator synthesizes their arguments into a clear decision space. You stay the judge.

The Deliberation Process

1

The Inquiry

A complex query or dilemma is presented to the grand chamber.

2

Agent Analysis

Each specialized AI mind analyzes the query through their unique persona and reasoning framework.

3

Active Debate

The minds exchange arguments, cite evidence, and challenge each other's logical fallacies in real-time.

4

The Verdict

The Moderator synthesizes the debate into a comprehensive, nuanced final resolution.
