## Query
how can i analyze composite materials on simulation, which program is the best?

## Sage Synthesis
Composite material simulation is best approached by selecting software that supports layered/anisotropic modeling, damage mechanics, and user-defined constitutive models rather than generic FEA tools. Abaqus stands out for composites due to its built-in cohesive element capabilities, progressive failure models (Hashin/Puck), and robust interaction of materials elements—making it a strong candidate for delamination, ply-level damage, and impact analysis. ANSYS Mechanical with its composite module is also competitive, particularly when paired with the user's existing familiarity from your wiki queries about pressure and structural projects.

## Analogies
- **geology**: layered sediment formation → composites behave like geological strata—each ply has distinct orientation and properties; simulation tools must model these as heterogeneous layers rather than homogenous blocks.
- **architecture**: timber frame structure vs monolithic concrete → just as a timber frame requires per-member analysis while concrete is modeled monolithically, composite layups demand software that resolves directional stiffness differences.
